The seven-case shortcut

Nominative names the subject. Genitive often marks absence, possession, quantity, and negation. Dative marks receivers. Accusative marks many direct objects and destinations. Instrumental marks tools and "with." Locative appears after specific prepositions. Vocative addresses someone directly.

02 · Nouns and adjectives

Gender tells endings
where to start.

Before choosing a case ending, identify gender and number. Adjectives usually follow the noun's gender, number, and case.

Masculine personal

dobry student

People like student, brat, and lekarz matter for accusative and plural agreement.

Masculine non-personal

dobry film

Inanimate masculine nouns often keep nominative-looking accusative forms: Widzę dobry film.

Feminine

dobra kawa

Many feminine nouns end in -a. Accusative often changes -a to -ę: piję dobrą kawę.

Neuter

dobre miasto

Neuter nouns commonly end in -o or -e and keep the same nominative and accusative form.

Nom: dobry student Gen: dobrego studenta Dat: dobremu studentowi Acc: dobrego studenta Inst: dobrym studentem Loc: dobrym studencie Voc: dobry studencie

03 · Verbs and triggers

Verbs choose meaning.
Cases show the roles.

Intermediate Polish depends on noticing what triggers a case: a verb, a preposition, a negator, or a motion/location contrast.

Choose a trigger

Imperfective

czytam, piszę, robię

Use for ongoing, habitual, repeated, or process-focused actions: Czytam książkę can mean "I am reading" or "I read."

Perfective

przeczytam, napiszę, zrobię

Use for bounded completed or future outcomes: Przeczytam książkę means "I will read the book through."

04 · Word order

Cases carry roles.
Order manages emphasis.

Polish often uses subject-verb-object order, but case endings allow movement. Put familiar material earlier and the new or contrastive point later.

Neutral

Ala czyta książkę.

The subject comes first, then the verb, then an accusative object.

Object as topic

Książkę czyta Ala.

The case ending keeps książkę as the object even when it appears first.

Negation

Ala nie czyta książki.

Negated direct objects commonly appear in genitive, so książka becomes książki.
